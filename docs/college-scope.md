# Final college feature scope

Scope closed on 13 October 2026 under the approved seven-task finish plan. Repair-return verification and its live walkthrough are complete; Smit confirmed all five walkthrough steps worked. This is human confirmation, separate from the automated evidence.

## Included functionality

- Administrator-created accounts, authentication, sessions and role/ownership safeguards.
- Asset registration, directories, profiles and maintenance history.
- Complaint reporting, review reasons, comments, scoped withdrawal and controlled reopening.
- Repair assignment, pre-start reassignment, technician start/completion, administrator return with reason, assigned-technician correction/resubmission and exact-submission verification.
- Immutable submission/review history, preserved original timestamps and complaint resolution only after verification.
- Technician workload visibility and role dashboards/navigation.
- Inspection scheduling/assignment/results, independent safety holds and evidence-backed administrator clearance.
- Preventive schedule creation, pause/activation and the existing completion-date-plus-frequency recurrence.
- Existing transaction/audit, migration, concurrency, account, availability and form-recovery protections.

Preserve every delivered feature. Availability precedence and overlapping repairs/independent holds remain authoritative. Correction does not broaden reassignment or reopen verified repairs.

## Future scope, excluded from this college version

Further cancellation/reopening/assignment policies; deadlines, blocked work and escalation; preventive work orders/history or recurrence changes; inspection checklists/automatic corrective work; structured location hierarchies and separate audit-management UI; notifications/reminders/calendar; attachments/QR; bulk import/export and additional in-app reports/advanced pagination; costs/labour/downtime, purchasing/warranties, spare inventory, technician skills/capacity planning, vendors, checkout/transfers, integrations and personalization.

These are possible future ideas, not unchecked college completion obligations. Existing audit/history/concurrency/availability protections remain included. Deployment, pre-deployment preparation, hosting/shared-backend work and production-only preparation remain excluded.
